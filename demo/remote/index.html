Real PTY · shared and disposable

Make a mess in a tiny shared shell.

This page connects the real lit-shell UI to a writable shell in one tiny Render container. Everyone shares its files and processes until the environment resets five minutes later.

⌁

Public, writable, and deliberately messy.

Other visitors can inspect, change, or delete your files and stop your processes. Never enter secrets or personal data. A root-owned gateway enforces the CAPTCHA, four-terminal cap, resource limits, and global five-minute reset.
