Real PTY · explicit opt-in

Try a real, deliberately tiny shell.

This page connects the real lit-shell UI to one isolated, read-only shell for at most 60 seconds. Starting it is anonymous and does not require an account.

⌁

No writes, network, secrets, or persistence.

A separate user, read-only chroot, syscall filter, and strict process, CPU, input, output, idle, and wall-clock limits protect the demo. Only one visitor is admitted at a time.

On-demand free service

Remote shell

Nothing has connected. The free service may need about a minute to wake.

The shell starts only after you press the button.

No analytics, login request, or background wake-up runs first.

Try id, env, ls /, cat /README.txt, uname -a, or date. Commands outside the fixed sandbox cannot be installed or reached.
